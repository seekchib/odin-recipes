Mushroom Spaghetti

Description

This easy sauce comes together in under 20 minutes. Include your choice of protein. The sauce has a kick from the dried red chilli peppers. To offset the heat, consider adding fresh mozzarella or parmesean to the finshed dish.

Ingredients

  • 15 oz canned tomatoes
  • 2 tbs tomato paste
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 3 fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbs olive oil
  • 40 grams of white mushrooms, sliced

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, crush canned tomatoes with hands.
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, add the olive oil.
  3. Once shimmering, cook the garlic until fragrant, about a minute and a half.
  4. Add sliced mushrooms and cook until tender
  5. Add the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute, stirring.
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste, and heat until bubbling.
  7. While sauce is heating, thinly slice the basil.
  8. Once sauce is bulbbling, remove from heat and add basil and stir.
  9. Serve over pasta of your choice.
